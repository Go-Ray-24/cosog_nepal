What am I looking at?

Each dot is a heat detection from a NASA satellite (VIIRS / MODIS), not a photo of flames — usually 1–3 hours old.

FRP (Fire Radiative Power, in MW) is the fire's heat output: bigger dot = hotter fire. Confidence is how sure the satellite is it's a real fire.
